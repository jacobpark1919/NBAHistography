About the Site

Hoopstography is a visual and interactive timeline of professional basketball history, spanning from the sport's earliest organized roots in 1894 to the present day. Every dot is a real event — zoom in to explore eras, hover to read.

Heavily inspired by Histography.io by Matan Stauber, a stunning interactive timeline of all of human history sourced from Wikipedia.

Data Sources

NBA.com/history — The official NBA historical record, scraped and structured into 1,400+ milestone events covering championships, records, rule changes, expansions, and drafts.

Hoopsrewind.app — A daily NBA history trivia game sharing the same event database. Events are deduplicated against the NBA.com dataset to avoid overlap.
